Implementation notes

  • Send line: a sentence ("Started", "Started 3 agents", "Sent a follow-up to") plus one chip per child. The chip reuses the file-chip look from ToolCallSummary (rounded bg-content/6 px-1, here text-[13px] text-content/80) with HarnessIcon and a trailing state icon (LoaderCircle with .zen-tool-spin, Check, CircleX). Model and harness are in the chip's tooltip; the task text and raw ids open below on the .zen-phase-step rail.
  • Digest: a HandoffMiniCard-style surface (rounded-lg border-content/8 bg-content/4). Header only when there are two or more updates: stacked glyphs, count, and "N needs you" in amber-400. Rows sort blocked, failed, stopped, settled, and are a grid of status icon, glyph, name text-sm text-content/85, model or status word text-[12px] text-content/40, one-line preview text-content/50, then a chevron or, for blocked, an amber action.
  • Preview text: settled and stopped show the end of excerpt (its last line, since the excerpt is the tail of the reply); failed shows error; blocked shows label.
  • Opened row: UpdateReply's line-clamp-4 and "Show more", "Open" and "Copy reply" as ghost buttons, raw ids in a mono text-[11px] text-content/35 line.
  • Replaces: AssignmentCard with the send line; the "Session updates" label and SessionUpdateCard with the digest, rendered at the head of the resumed turn rather than in a user bubble.
  • Child tab: hairline dividers (bg-content/10, text-xs text-content/45) above a normal UserMessageBlock bubble when the block has acceptedAssignment, and after the turn footer once the outcome was delivered.
  • Trade-off: scales best when the operator runs several children at once and puts what needs you first, but it adds a bordered surface, and a one-child turn pays for a box it does not need.
