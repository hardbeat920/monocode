The native callers produced English relative-time labels and approximated locale collation with lowercase or code-point comparisons. The retained application uses `Intl.RelativeTimeFormat` with `numeric: "auto"` and `String.localeCompare`. The [source hashes](before-source-sha256.json) identify that compiled old-source snapshot.

The [extended BEFORE log](extended-before.log) records twenty failing caller regressions and two passing preservation cases across six targets. It includes MCP and skill pickers, saved-note UI order, the actual Inbox formatter, and the root-owned SCM, notification-project, notes-view and composer-skill fixtures. JavaScript rounding and stable note-picker ties passed. The separate [secondary-page BEFORE log](pages-before.log) then reproduced the English phrase where French was expected, while its rounding fixture passed. The fixtures check score, UTF-16 length, availability, pin, scope and recency priorities alongside locale ties. The [runner](check-extended-before.sh) used the established workspace library and binary graph with all features, locked offline dependencies, two jobs and no fail-fast.

The [Node oracle](intl-oracle.jsonl) records the exact phrases, punctuation order and canonical Unicode comparisons from Node 25.9.0 with ICU 78.3. Its [script](intl-oracle.js) only formats and compares literal fixture strings. French, Japanese and Arabic cases cover past and future special day phrases and ordinary hour phrases. The formatter keeps every retained rounding step and the thresholds of 60 seconds, 60 minutes, 24 hours, seven days, 4.34524 weeks and twelve months.

The caller patch delegates collation to the shared native ICU crate. The Inbox view delegates relative time to the engine. Secondary notes and automation pages pass their numeric timestamps to the same rounding formatter without an ISO conversion. Real view calls select the OS locale. Controlled tests select a locale only on their own thread, with no environment or ICU global mutation. Invalid formatter results retain the empty-string behavior of the retained catch block.
